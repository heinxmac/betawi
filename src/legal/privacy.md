## The short version

There are no accounts, no advertising trackers and no analytics. If you ask for a table, your name, mobile number and choices reach us so we can confirm your table. We use them for nothing else.

## Who we are

Betawi Restaurant, Lake Terrace Tower, Cluster D, JLT, Dubai. Phone 04 454 2329.

## When you send a table request

"Send request" sends the number of guests, your seating choice, the date and time, your name, your mobile number and any note. They are saved in a private Google Sheet and Google Calendar, and may also be added to a private Notion page, so we can manage tables. We get an alert in Telegram so we can message you on WhatsApp to confirm. Google, Notion and Telegram store this information for us under their own privacy policies. We keep a request only as long as we need it for your visit and our records, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Links to other sites

This site links to Google Maps, Talabat and Instagram. Nothing loads from them until you tap a link. Each has its own privacy policy.

## Where the site is hosted

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages, and table requests pass through Cloudflare on their way to us. Like most hosts, Cloudflare may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure. Fonts and images come from the site itself.
